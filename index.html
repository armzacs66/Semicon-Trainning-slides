01 · OXIDATIONBOC690042 · KMUTNB

Oxidation Process

การออกซิเดชัน · การสร้างชั้น SiO₂ บนผิวซิลิคอน
Gate oxide formation

Forms the insulating oxide between gate and channel that controls MOSFET current flow.

Batch furnace

Vertically stacks production and dummy wafers and exposes them to reactant gases at high temperature.

Wet oxidation

H₂O vapour at atmospheric pressure, giving the fastest oxide growth rate.

Dry oxidation

O₂ (often diluted with N₂) at atmospheric pressure: a moderate rate and higher film density.

Radical oxidation

H₂ + O₂ below 1 Torr. Slow, but with superior control and conformal film quality.

Why radicals

Uniform thickness on (100) and (110) Si (a = b), rounded stress corners, and they even oxidise stable Si₃N₄.

02 · PHOTOLITHOGRAPHYBOC690042 · KMUTNB

Photolithography Process

โฟโตลิโทกราฟี · การถ่ายแบบลวดลายด้วยแสง
Like a cookie cutter stamping identical shapes: cookie cutter = photomask, cover = mask, and making the mask is the photo process.
Photoresist (PR)

Coated on the wafer, exposed through a photomask, then developed. Light changes its solubility in the developer.

Positive vs negative

Positive: exposed areas dissolve, suiting fine patterns. Negative: exposed areas cure and remain but swell.

Batch spraying

Like colouring many snacks on one board at once: identical patterns quickly, without colouring each by hand.

Larger lens → higher NA

Collects more diffracted light (3–5 rays), raising the numerical aperture and improving resolution.

ArF immersion + top coat

Water replaces air to raise the refractive index and NA, and a waterproof top coat protects the PR. Finer patterns at the same 193 nm.

03 · ETCHBOC690042 · KMUTNB

Etch Process

การกัด · ขุดลวดลายลงในชั้นวัสดุ
Etching

The PR mold protects the pattern. Material reacts only where it is unmasked, digging the pattern into the layer.

Filling

Like chocolate syrup poured into the hollow: the trench is filled and the excess cleared before the next layer.

Isotropic

Etches in all directions and undercuts below the PR. High selectivity and speed, but poor precision.

Anisotropic

Etches vertically only and keeps the pattern as drawn. Lower selectivity and speed.

Selectivity

Gas and plasma conditions decide which material is removed, enabling selective etching of specific layers.

04 · DEPOSITIONBOC690042 · KMUTNB

Deposition · Step Coverage

การสะสมฟิล์มบาง · ความครอบคลุมขั้นบันไดและการเติมช่อง
Deposition

Chocolate syrup is applied into the etched hollow, and a new cookie layer is laid over it.

Purpose

Thin films of different materials are added layer by layer on the wafer, enabling miniaturisation.

Step coverage

Sidewall (b/a) and bottom (c/a) film thickness relative to the top. The closer to 1, the more uniform.

Poor coverage

Thinner sidewall and bottom films (ratio below 1), since deposition can't be guaranteed on uneven surfaces.

Gap fill

Poor fill pinches off the trench opening and traps voids. Excellent fill leaves almost none.

04 · DEPOSITIONBOC690042 · KMUTNB

CVD vs ALD

การสะสมไอเคมี เทียบกับ การสะสมชั้นอะตอม
Conventional CVD

Source and reactant gases are supplied together, so the film grows continuously and fast through adsorption, surface diffusion and reaction.

Atomic Layer Deposition (ALD)

Source, purge, reactant and purge gases are supplied in sequence, so one cycle deposits exactly one atomic layer.

05 · METALLIZATIONBOC690042 · KMUTNB

Metallization · Interconnect

การเชื่อมต่อโลหะ · Metal Interconnection
Process order

Devices are formed first in FEOL at the bottom. Manufacturing then proceeds upward through BEOL, where Cu layers and vias connect device to device.

Role

Metallization gives the chip its purpose by wiring billions of isolated devices into working circuits.

I/O terminals

The top layer ends in input/output terminals such as solder bumps for packaging.

Memory cell array

Word lines and bit lines are the metal wiring that links each cell to its control signals.

06 · TESTBOC690042 · KMUTNB

Test & Reliability

การทดสอบและความน่าเชื่อถือ
Purpose

The main goal of testing is to prevent defective products from shipping.

Test types
TEMPHot · Cold · RoomSPEEDCore · SpeedOPERATIONDC · AC · Function
Test system

Signals pass from the test system through the tester head and probe card to the wafer on the prober, and a cooler controls temperature.

Bathtub curve

Failure rate is high early (manufacturing defects), low and steady during useful life (random defects), and rises again at wear-out.

Burn-in

Temperature and voltage stress exposes latent defects in advance, so early failures are screened out before shipment.

07 · PACKAGEBOC690042 · KMUTNB

Semiconductor Package

บรรจุภัณฑ์เซมิคอนดักเตอร์ · ระดับ บทบาท และแนวโน้ม
Package levels

L0 cut chips from the wafer · L1 package one chip · L2 mount on a module or card · L3 card on the motherboard. COB skips L1.

Package forms

Most packages connect externally through solder balls (FBGA) or leads (TSOP), which serve as the pins.

Four roles
Mechanical protectionElectrical connectionMechanical connectionHeat dissipation

EMC encases the chip to shield it from mechanical and chemical shock.

Development trends
Heat dissipationHigh speed3D stackingSmall form factorHigh reliabilityLow cost
Development process

Chip design → feasibility review → package design (electrical, thermal, structural analysis) → fabrication → characterisation and reliability tests. Results feed back to chip designers.

07 · PACKAGEBOC690042 · KMUTNB

Package Type I

ประเภทบรรจุภัณฑ์ I · Through-hole, SMT, BGA, LGA
Through-hole · DIP, ZIP

Leads are inserted into PCB holes. DIP has two rows of pins and ZIP a single zig-zag row.

Surface mount · QFP/TQFP, TSOP, SOJ

Leads attach to the PCB surface: four sides (QFP), two sides gull-wing (TSOP) or J-shaped (SOJ), allowing more pins.

BGA / FBGA

Solder balls are attached to the package substrate. This is the most common form of substrate-type package.

LGA

The substrate has only flat ball lands with no solder balls, and it is becoming more common.

Difference

BGA has solder balls on the substrate, while LGA has bare lands. Flip the BGA/LGA views to see the underside.

07 · PACKAGEBOC690042 · KMUTNB

Package Type II · Flip Chip

ประเภทบรรจุภัณฑ์ II · Wire bonding เทียบกับ Flip chip
Standard FBGA

The chip is connected to the substrate by bonding wires, so the signal takes a longer path.

Flip chip

Bumps are formed on the chip's pads, then the chip is flipped and attached directly to the substrate: a short, direct path.

Benefit

Shorter wiring improves electrical performance and lets the package be thinner.

07 · PACKAGEBOC690042 · KMUTNB

Chip Stacking · TSV · HBM

การเรียงซ้อนชิป · Wire bonding, TSV, HBM และ 2.5D
Wire bonding stack

Stacked chips are connected by wires, and capacity grows with the number of chips.

TSV stack

Vertical through-silicon electrodes replace wires, giving better performance and a smaller package.

HBM stack

DRAM dies are stacked vertically and connected through TSVs and micro-bumps, so signals pass between dies without wires.

2.5D structure

The DRAM stack and logic die sit side by side on an interposer (PHY areas), which connects to the package substrate through bumps and BGA balls.

07 · PACKAGEBOC690042 · KMUTNB

Monolithic vs Chiplet

ชิปเล็ตและการรวมระบบ · SoC เทียบกับ SiP
Monolithic

A single comprehensive chip integrates all functional logic blocks (CPU, GPU, modem, SRAM).

Chiplet

Functional blocks are split into separate dies, each on its best-fit process node, then integrated through advanced packaging like TSV.

SoC

All functions on a single chip laid out in a plane, so signals travel a long horizontal path.

SiP stacked with TSV

Multiple chips are stacked vertically in one package and connected through TSVs, so the signal path is much shorter.

08 · TCADBOC690042 · KMUTNB

TCAD · Technology CAD

การจำลองกระบวนการผลิตและอุปกรณ์ด้วยคอมพิวเตอร์
TCAD is a branch of EDA that uses physics-based computer simulations to model semiconductor fabrication processes and the electrical behaviour of devices.
Process TCAD

Simulates foundry steps (ion implantation, diffusion, oxidation, deposition, etching, mechanical stress) to predict the wafer's physical structure.

Device TCAD

Solves the Poisson, continuity and drift-diffusion equations on a discretised mesh to simulate current flow and thermal behaviour.

Interconnect TCAD

Models the non-active parts of ICs to extract parasitics and evaluate signal interference and RC effects.
